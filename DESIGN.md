# FC Altynka design system

The user approved the Airofit material iteration and requested its implementation. This direction replaces the earlier Floating Glass design, including its ambient gradient background and sliding navigation lens.

## References and intent

Dropset supplies the quiet dark foundation and clear hierarchy. Airofit supplies coloured curved surfaces, inner edge lighting and pale controls. Revolut informs the separation of primary actions, obligations and account details. The approved visual source is `artifacts/reference-redesign/airofit.html`; implementation lives in `apps/miniapp/src/styles/app.css`, with theme overrides in `apps/miniapp/src/styles/themes.css`.

The page background stays solid: `#08090b` in the default dark theme and `#f4f2f7` in the selectable light theme. Colour and gradients belong to individual surfaces. There is no backdrop blur. The account's explicit theme preference controls the app and supported Telegram header/background/bar colours; Telegram theme changes do not override it. A local cache applies the preference before account loading. Light mode uses dark ink, pale neutral surfaces and a pale dock, while plum/blue/coral material cards keep their light text and coloured identity. The one exception to the solid background is the favourite-club background (see «Favourite club»): the club pattern behind the whole page, faded enough for page text to stay readable.

## Favourite club

A player may pick a favourite club or national team from a 25-entry catalogue (`packages/core/src/clubs.ts`); without a choice nothing below applies and the app looks exactly as before. The club colour only recolours the accent: `--accent`, `--accent-ink`, `--on-accent`, `--accent-soft`, the focus ring, text selection, `.btn.primary` outside coloured materials, links and chips on the page background, and the selected dock item. Wherever the accent is used as a text colour (`.btn-text`, `.system-action-label`, section-heading links such as «Все задачи», the selected dock label) it is `--accent-ink`, which a core test holds to 4.5:1 on the page and on cards (`#19191f` dark, `#ffffff` light). In the dock the selected label and icon take `--accent-ink` in both themes; in the light theme the selected icon also sits on a `--accent-soft` circle and the central menu button becomes the club accent with `onAccent` icon, while in the dark theme the selected circle stays the dock's pale tint and the central button stays pearl. Materials, the buttons on them, `--warn`, `--danger`, medals and the podium keep their own colours, and Telegram's header/background/bar colours stay the theme background. Light and dark themes differ only in the accent trio (`accent`, `onAccent`, `accentInk`), each held to 4.5:1 by a core test.

**Background.** `Screen` renders a `.club-backdrop` into `body`: fixed to the whole viewport like wallpaper (content scrolls over it), the club pattern at full strength everywhere and the crest at 0.2 near the top. The pattern opacity is per club and theme (`--club-pattern-opacity`, computed by `clubVariables`): at most 0.55 in the dark theme and 0.3 in the light one, lowered in 0.01 steps until the page `--text` and `--hint` keep 4.5:1 over every drawn pattern colour, because every piece of page text (headings, the weekday on the match screen, section headings) sits on the pattern. Pale patterns therefore fade in the dark theme (white, yellow, sky blue: about 0.24–0.43) and saturated ones in the light theme (about 0.10–0.16). A test checks all clubs in both themes. In the dark theme the title and subtitle also carry a soft dark text shadow as a margin. The background is `pointer-events: none` and `aria-hidden`, never appears in sheets and is hidden under `prefers-contrast: more`; there only the colour stays, and `.btn.primary` outside coloured materials is a flat club-accent button without gradient or shadow.

**Heading crest.** A small crest (about 30×36 px, `aria-hidden`) sits at the right of the `Screen` heading, only when the screen has no `action`. Negative block margins keep it from making the heading taller than its action variant, so skeletons do not shift.

**Profile card.** `PlayerSportsCard` swaps `material-plum` for `.club-material`, a plum-style volumetric surface in the club's `material` colours. It is one material for both themes, like the other coloured cards. There is no crest on the card (the heading already carries it); a visually hidden «Любимый клуб: <название>» names the club for screen readers. Everywhere in the app the rating ring's inner disc meets the ring with no gap. On the club card the rating ring sits on a dark disc so tier colours stay readable on pale pearl cards. Other plum surfaces (match card, summary) stay plum. The loading skeleton of one's own profile (the Profile screen, or one's own row opened from a list) uses the cached club so the colour does not jump.

**Picker.** An `ActionSheet` «Любимый клуб» opened from the row «Любимый клуб» in «Настройка профиля» (route `/profile/club`). Tabs «Клубы | Сборные»; under «Клубы» a row of league chips (`.club-leagues`, scrolls sideways on purpose and is listed in `HORIZONTAL_SCROLLERS`); then «Без клуба» with the hint «Обычная тема» (named by its label, the hint is its description); then a grid of three tiles per row (also at 320 px) with crest and name, the selected tile outlined in the club colour with a check and `aria-pressed`; a footer note «Список клубов будет пополняться». A pick applies at once, closes the sheet and saves in the background, reverting with a toast on failure. The sheet opens on the tab and league of the current choice.

## Materials

- Plum: the first upcoming match and match details. A pink upper rim, deep plum centre and soft curved lower edge.
- Blue: the next match, scheduling tasks and club profile settings. A lavender upper edge, dark blue centre and softly lit corners.
- Coral: contribution tasks and personal payment. Dark warm centre and coral lower edge.
- Graphite: archive lists and secondary management content.

Each material is opaque. Inner shadows simulate the user-requested curved edge. Body text remains light over quiet dark portions. Colour identifies an object/category; it does not imply overdue, bank verification or success.

Primary buttons use a pale pearly fill with dark text. Coral surfaces receive a warm pearl variant. Secondary buttons are darker. Buttons have at least 48 px height, pill corners, visible focus and short press feedback. Destructive actions retain explicit text and confirmation.

## Name

The product is called «ФК Алтынка» everywhere people read it: the page title, the error screen, interface copy and the bot's Telegram name and descriptions (`scripts/bot-profile.mjs`). «ФК Алтынка» is not declined («Открывай ФК Алтынка…»). Latin spelling remains only in code and link names (`fc_altynka_bot`, `fc_altynka_app`, `@fc_altynka_news`); the toss coin is engraved «ФК АЛТЫНКА • 2026 •».

## Typography and frame

Apple system stack with bundled Onest Variable fallback, optical sizing enabled. Screen titles 28 px / 600, 27 px below 360 px; section headings 18 px / 550; body 16 px; metadata 12–14 px. Navigation labels are 12 px; 12 px is the text floor everywhere. The home match cards lead with one 44 px / 550 answer (38 px on narrow phones) under a quiet 14 px date. Dates, amounts and roster counts use tabular figures.

One centred column caps at 440 px, with 20 px gutters and 16 px below 360 px. Content groups use 20 px gaps. Cards use approximately 30 px corners; the featured match uses 37 px and 21 px padding. Device and Telegram safe areas are included. On the four tabs at least 148 px plus bottom inset is reserved for the fixed dock; deeper screens have no dock and keep 40 px plus bottom inset (126 px when a fixed contextual action is shown).

## Navigation and screens

Nested screens support Back with an inward horizontal swipe starting within 24 px of either canvas edge. The gesture shares the Telegram Back action; it never uses external browser history. A small arrow follows the pull and changes tone at the commit threshold; it starts half beyond the edge inside a clipping layer, so a pull from the right edge never widens the page into a horizontal scroll. Root tabs stay in place. Vertical scrolling, carousels, tabs, inputs, cancellation and multitouch retain their own behavior. An open creation sheet owns Back and blocks it while saving. Reduced motion uses a stationary arrow.

Screen changes are animated with the browser's View Transitions API (`lib/pageTransition.ts`, `styles/transitions.css`). A deeper screen (list → match, menu → section, match → review) arrives 28 px from the right while the previous one fades and drifts left, 280 ms on `--ease-out`; Back mirrors it in 240 ms. Dock tabs cross-fade in place in about 170 ms, without travel. Toasts stay still during every change; the dock stays still between tabs, sinks 28 px and fades out in 180 ms when a deeper screen opens, and rises back in 240 ms on Back to a tab. A sheet with its own route keeps its own slide and plays no page transition. Match tabs (Участники, Составы, Игры, Оплата) slide only their panel, 24 px toward the side of the chosen tab in 220 ms, while the heading, the match card, the tab bar, the fixed contextual action stay still; the action cross-fades when it changes. Touching a tab loads its code, so the panel slides in with its content rather than its skeleton. Back returns the list to the scroll position it was left at; a screen opened forward or from the dock starts at the top. Clients without view transitions (iOS below 18) and reduced motion change screens at once, with the same scroll rules.

Screens start directly with the page title at the standard safe-area inset. In full screen the title, back button and actions start below both the status bar and Telegram's floating «Закрыть» and «⋯» buttons, sheets stop below them, and a band of the page colour behind the status bar fades out across the button row, so scrolled content never runs into the clock or the buttons. There is no separate club header, rotating football greeting, logo or avatar above it. Profile editing actions appear directly below its summary; the profile title has no decorative icon or action on its right.

Everyone has the same dock: Главная, Матчи, a central menu button, Задачи, Профиль. The dock belongs to these four tabs only (`hasDock` in `lib/pageTransition.ts`), including the sheets that open over Profile. Any deeper screen (a match and its game, review or scheduling, Статистика, Игроки and player profiles, Площадки, the skills questionnaire) hides it until Back returns to a tab: like most mobile apps, a second- or third-level screen is about its task, and its own Back control, Telegram Back and the edge-back gesture are the way out. A crashed deeper screen shows «На главную» itself. The central button (nine dots; «Главная» already uses four tiles) opens the menu as a pop-up card, not a bottom sheet: a rounded card (28 px corners, up to 380 px wide) springs out of the button over 340 ms, sits 14 px above the dock and points at the button with a small caret, while the dots turn into a cross. The dock stays visible; the rest of the screen is dimmed. Sections without a dock slot are icons with a label under each, four per row, on the app's materials (62 px tile, 22 px corners; 54 px below 360 px). «Создать матч» is pearl and shown only to someone who can create a match; «Статистика» is plum, «Игроки» blue, «Площадки» coral. A tap outside the card (the dock included), Escape or Back closes it in 160 ms and focus returns to the central button; choosing an icon closes it at once. Reduced motion shows and hides it without animation. Creation and role/name management are role-gated. The player directory and sports profiles are available to every community member. The dock caps at 416 px, sits 16 px above the safe bottom, has 32 px corners and an opaque blue material. The central pearl control is 52 px. Selection lights the icon; there is no moving lens. A small dot identifies real pending tasks and is hidden if task refresh fails.

**Sheet and keyboard.** With the keyboard open the sheet stays anchored to the bottom of the screen and continues under the keys; it is never lifted, so no strip of the dimmed page shows between the sheet and the keyboard. `useBottomSheet` measures the visual viewport on every resize, scroll and focusout (and on Telegram `viewportChanged` once `isStateStable`): keyboard height = `innerHeight − visualViewport.height − visualViewport.offsetTop`, never below 0, recomputed rather than trusting `offsetTop` to return to 0 (iOS 26), applied once per animation frame as `--sheet-viewport` and `--sheet-keyboard`. The sheet pads its content and footer by the keyboard height, so the primary button sits just above the keys (the bottom safe-area inset gives way to the keyboard padding, `max(safe-bottom − pad, 0)`, so there is no extra gap above the keys), and its cap lets the box reach under them by exactly that height: the visible part never exceeds the visible viewport minus the top gap and the remembered height (`--sheet-min`, which never counts the padding) gives way the same way. Padding and cap move together over 250 ms on `cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)`, close to the iOS keyboard (registered `--sheet-pad`/`--sheet-visible`); never while dragging or closing, not at all with reduced motion; after the keyboard closes the sheet returns to its previous height through the same transition. Once the padding settles, and on focus inside the sheet, the focused field is scrolled into the visible part of `.sheet-content` with `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })` rules limited to that scroll area; the page itself is never scrolled. On Android, where Telegram shrinks the web view itself, the keyboard height comes out 0 and the sheet simply fits the new viewport, with the same transition.

**Sheet height.** An open sheet never changes height from what happens inside it: it is as tall as its tallest state from the start and never gets shorter while open. `ActionSheet` keeps the largest height the sheet has had: its natural height (drag area, header, the content's full scroll height, footer) in `--sheet-min` on the dialog, and CSS clamps it with the same expression as the max height (`min-height: min(calc(var(--sheet-min) + var(--sheet-pad)), var(--sheet-max))`, `--sheet-pad` being the keyboard padding described above), so the keyboard, rotation or a short screen still win and the sheet never leaves the screen. While the sheet is capped (its content scrolls) the box itself is remembered: a squeezed box under the keyboard never lowers the value, so the sheet comes back to its full height when the keyboard goes away, and a list that reached the cap keeps the sheet there when it gets shorter. A sheet with its own cap narrows `--sheet-max` rather than setting `max-height`, so the minimum respects it too. Heights come from layout (the used `height`), never from the drawn box, so a sheet scaled back under a nested one records nothing smaller. Closing forgets the height; a sheet that deliberately turns into another step (a confirmation becoming a result, a picker becoming a form) passes a new `step` and is measured afresh; a nested sheet is its own instance. Long lists still stop at the screen height and scroll inside. Content that appears later takes a place reserved in advance, one of three ways: content that appears after an action stands behind a placeholder (`SheetPlaceholder`): the real content drawn invisibly sets its exact size, with the corner radius of its neighbours, a 1 px dashed `--line` frame, no fill or accent and a centred hint in `--hint`, 14 px, plain text a screen reader reads; it is not pressable and takes no focus. Hints are short, «what to do → what appears», and a placeholder is never taller than the content it stands in for: over a one-line target (a goal row, an extra-expense row, a summary row) the hint is one line at 320 px, about 30 characters, and only a taller target may take two. A hint is shown only in the state it describes («Выбери группу — кто может играть» only while no group is chosen); in any other state the same place stays reserved, empty and without a frame (`SheetPlaceholder` without `hint`), as it does while the content loads; the new-match summary row does it through `SummaryLine mode="reserve"` while the summary loads or fails. An error that is expected in the normal flow (a taken name, a wrong number, a duplicate court) has its line reserved in advance: `.field-error-slot` is sized by the longest of its expected messages, written in `data-sizer`, and the alert (`role="alert"`, referenced by the field's `aria-describedby`) mounts inside it; a rare error is not reserved and may make room when it comes. Tabs and filters with panels of different length stack every panel in one grid cell (`.sheet-stack`): the area is as tall as the longest panel, inactive panels are `visibility: hidden` and `inert` (`.sheet-stack-hidden`), and a short panel leaves empty space below without a hint. Loading inside a sheet shows a skeleton as tall as the loaded content (see Skeletons), with a typical number of rows when the length depends on data. Content replacing a placeholder fades in place over 220 ms without travel (`.reveal`, applied through the shared `useRevealAfter` hook); reduced motion shows it at once. A stepped flow whose steps differ in height (the match rating: a person, a prize with one row per player, the summary) draws invisible copies of its tallest steps in the same grid cell, so it keeps one height from step to step and does not pass `step`. The copies only have to lift the sheet to its cap: in a capped sheet they are clipped to the room the stack has (`--review-fit`), so a step never scrolls into empty space below its card, while a prize list longer than the screen still scrolls. The rating footer holds only the buttons; the recap («Оценено игроков…», one `aria-live` line) and the rare «Чтобы отправить, оцени хотя бы одного игрока или матч» sit at the bottom of the final «Матч в целом» card, which has room for them, and the note appears only in the state it describes.

The new-match sheet fits date, optional comment and, only for a person who can create in more than one group, a native «Группа» select in one step. With a single creatable group there is no group field and the submit reads «Создать матч»; with several, the select lists every group, nothing is preselected and «Открыть запись» stays disabled until a group is chosen. A person with no groups at all sees the connect-bot instruction and «Добавить в группу» instead of the form; a person whose groups are all player-only sees «Создавать матчи может организатор группы…» (ask a group admin for the organizer role), the secondary line about connecting their own group, and the same button; failures preserve input. Success closes the sheet and opens Active matches. Browser Back may leave a pending request; its late response must not navigate the person away from their new screen.

While signup is open the match heading carries «Позвать в матч» (a share glyph, 44 px) before the organizer's «⋯». It opens Telegram's own forward chooser with a direct link to the match (`startapp=e<id>`) and one line: date, weekday, time and pitch, then «Записано N из M, есть места.» or «Основа заполнена, можно встать в резерв.». Finished and cancelled matches have no invite.

«Иду» with a free place in the main roster shows «Ты в основе» and the person's row at once, with the success haptic, and sends one request; a failed request restores the previous state and the inline error with «Повторить». Touching an in-app link starts loading the match behind it and the code of on-demand screens, so the screen transition usually lands on content rather than a skeleton.

Match details lead with day/month and weekday, followed by one plum card: prominent time, venue, roster count, short match status and personal participation. A venue without a link or a hand-typed place stays plain text. Participation remains visible on Участники, Составы and Оплата; the existing list/teams/bill query values remain compatible. There is no permanent step indicator. The organizer note sits before the roster. Main and reserve lists remain separate; a reservist sees their queue position. Изменить opens an explicit exit action; organizer cancellation stays in a separate confirmed menu, and player management opens from each row's contextual menu.

One contextual primary action is fixed at the bottom of the screen, 16 px above the safe bottom where the tabs keep their dock: join/reserve, schedule, split/publish teams, or open/submit the bill form. Scheduling takes priority for an organizer while collecting; their own signup remains available in the card. Content has extra bottom clearance when this action exists. Amounts can be copied through the adjacent copy affordance. Closed bills expose no new transfer/mark actions, and receivers have no personal obligation card. Expenses and payer lines follow. A payment mark is the member's declaration, not bank verification; correcting paid lines retains existing receiver/organizer permissions.

Time and duration are required before a new bill can be issued, enforced server-side and explained with an appointment action in the bill tab. Venue is optional. Scheduling is read-only after billing/cancellation/closure. Bill creation and editing open in a native bottom sheet over the unchanged Payment tab. The form scrolls independently, and its submit footer remains at the bottom of the sheet, above keyboard/safe-area clearance. Pending submission locks dismissal; closing restores trigger focus. Drafts preserve amounts on refresh and only submit currently present selected signups. Closed matches and legacy bills remain readable. Team publication rejects a stale roster or closed match. Transient notifications appear at the safe top inset throughout the app, above content and clear of the fixed action and bottom navigation. A lost connection shows one graphite notice in the same place instead of an error on every screen: an offline glyph, «Нет связи» and «Обновим, когда сеть вернётся» on one line. It appears when the browser goes offline or when a request gets no answer at all (the phone often reports a connection that does not work); any answer from the server, an error included, clears it.
