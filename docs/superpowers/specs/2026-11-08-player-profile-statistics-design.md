# Player profile statistics — approved design

The user approved the iterated local profile and explicitly authorized implementation, merging into main and production deployment on 8 November 2026. The latest review artifact is `artifacts/player-stats-preview/profile-large-avatar-role-first-dark.png`; the extra date tile at the right of played-meeting rows must be omitted.

The profile retains its settings, initial setup, navigation, verified Telegram identity/photo and current rating formula. Use one plum card: larger avatar, name, compact role emoji before Telegram username, continuous rating ring without a visible caption, and an inset sports panel whose heading is the player's position. Five cells, in order: goals, assists (explicitly initialized to zero), short games, win percentage, mirrored insole silhouettes. Working insole opacity 1; inactive .25; both inactive when unknown. Apple Color Emoji is native on Apple systems; other platforms use native emoji fallback. Roles: player ⚽️, organizer 🧑‍✈️, admin 🧑‍💻. Rating colours follow inspected FACEIT assets: level 1 grey, 2–3 green, 4–7 yellow, 8–9 orange, 10 red; decimals keep their precise value, integer part picks colour, whole values omit decimal zero.

Show six agreed achievements: first goal, three goals in one meeting, first short-game win, ten total goals, ten short games and ten meetings. Show played meetings with their date once, venue, goals/games/results, available review score, and a sheet of that player's short games. Preserve review history for legacy meetings without a game protocol. These blocks appear in own and peer profiles; personal settings appear only in own profile.

The authoritative store is `events.game_state_json`. Derive per-user statistics from frozen roster userId and recorded games, including the existing 6 November session. Every short game played by the user's frozen team counts; there is no substitution tracking. Unknown scorers do not receive attribution; guests without userId are not guessed by name. Editing/undo/retry cannot inflate counters because nothing is accumulated separately. Cancelled/legacy/unstarted sessions do not produce statistics. Own history retains former groups; foreign statistics and history are restricted to active shared groups. Totals include all eligible sessions, displayed history the latest 20. Aggregates and per-meeting best goals must not depend on review voting deadlines or history truncation.

No database migration or production writes are needed for statistics. Assist recording and guest-to-account linking are outside this release; assists show zero by explicit user instruction. Production verification must compare event/protocol data before and after deploy, check actual profiles and authenticated/unauthenticated API behavior, and preserve existing match/team/payment behavior.
